Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Your email address, the name you choose, and the entries, accounts, budgets and goals you and your partner add to your household.

How it is used

Only to run Coupledger for your household. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

Members of your household. Each household’s data is separated from every other household by database access rules.

Bank details

Coupledger never asks for bank logins and does not hold or move real money.

Your choices

You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time by writing to hi@amortree.com.

Service providers

Your data is stored with our database and sign-in provider (Supabase). If we add hosting or analytics providers, they will be listed here.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
