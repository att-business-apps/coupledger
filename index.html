Why we built it

Coupledger began as the ledger we made for our own household, because splitting “who paid for what” should never feel like an argument.

We kept it calm, quick to use, and built for two.

Questions

Good to know.

Do we both need an account?+

Yes. Each of you signs up with your own email, and your partner joins your household with a one-time invite code or link. After that you both see the same entries, balances and budgets.

How much does it cost?+

You can sign up and explore in your browser right now. If paid plans are introduced, they will be announced on this page first.

Does it connect to my bank?+

No. You add entries yourselves. Coupledger never asks for bank logins and never holds or moves real money. It is a tracker for your household.

Is our data private?+

Each household’s data is kept separate from every other household by database access rules, so only the two of you can see it. Sign-in uses your email and password, with a reset link if you forget.

What is Kakeibo?+

Kakeibo is a Japanese household ledger habit. You plan the month with savings first, sort spending into Needs, Wants, Culture and Extra, and look back at month end. Coupledger is built around those three habits, and you log entries yourselves, which is part of the point.

Which devices does it work on?+

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer: Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox. On a phone, use “Add to Home Screen” and it opens like an app. An Android app is coming soon.

What if I add something by mistake?+

Edit it, or delete it and tap Undo within five seconds to bring it back.

Can more than two people share a household?+

Coupledger is built for couples, so a household is for two people for now.
